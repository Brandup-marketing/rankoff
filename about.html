Why it exists

Rankoff started with one question.

What if a business could buy the top spot in the open, not in a hidden ad auction? What if everyone could see who is on top, what it cost, and who is getting the clicks?

Rankoff is the answer: a public board where the price, the position and the clicks are all on show, and every listing is labelled as sponsored.
