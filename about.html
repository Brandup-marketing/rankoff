The story behind the board

Attention has a price.
Make it visible.

RANKOFF is a public sponsored leaderboard where products compete for the attention they earn. One board, one clear rule: the highest valid bid takes the highest sponsored position.

Why it exists

Rankoff started with one question.

What if a product launch had a visible market instead of a hidden ad slot? What if anyone could see who was winning, what the position cost, and how much attention the board generated?

Rankoff turns that question into a public place to compete. Listings are clear, bids are visible, and sponsored placement is labelled as sponsored.
