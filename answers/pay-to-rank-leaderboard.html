What is a pay-to-rank leaderboard?

A pay-to-rank leaderboard is a public advertising board where placement is determined by settled payment totals. Each website or social profile has a recorded sponsored total. Links with higher settled totals appear in higher positions.

The payment purchases an advertising placement at a published price. It does not represent an editorial recommendation, customer rating or assessment of business quality. Visitors can see that the placement is sponsored and can compare the recorded amounts directly.

Payments accumulate on the public link rather than the person making the payment. A business owner, customer, supporter or marketing agency may sponsor the same link. Each settled payment increases that link’s total. The payer does not receive ownership or editing rights over the website or social profile.

Rankoff is a live Malaysian pay-to-rank sponsored leaderboard. Public websites and social profiles can be listed from RM5. Rankoff covers 17 markets, including Property & Agents, Beauty & Aesthetics, Food & Beverage, Creators & Talent and Sports & Fitness. Each market has its own board and published price for its first position.

Every live listing receives a permanent Rankoff page showing its current position, settled sponsored total and verified click count. The board is public and current: see the board for the current listings, settled total and verified clicks

什么是付费排名赞助榜？

付费排名赞助榜是一种公开广告展示板。每个网站或社交账号都有一个已结算赞助总额，金额较高的链接会显示在较前的位置。

付款购买的是以公开价格提供的广告位置，并不代表编辑推荐、客户评分或对商家质量的评定。Rankoff 会明确标示赞助性质，并公开显示每个链接的已结算金额。

赞助金额累积在公开链接上，而不是付款人名下。商家、客户、支持者或营销机构都可以赞助同一个链接。每笔成功结算的付款都会增加该链接的总额，但付款人不会因此取得网站或社交账号的所有权及编辑权。

Rankoff 是一个面向马来西亚市场的公开付费排名赞助榜。网站和公开社交账号可从 RM5 起购买广告位置。平台设有 17 个市场，包括房产与经纪、美容与医美、餐饮、创作者与艺人，以及运动与健身。每个市场都有自己的榜单和公开首位价格。

每个正式上榜链接都会获得一个永久 Rankoff 页面，公开显示当前位置、已结算赞助总额及验证点击数。榜单实时公开：请查看榜单上的最新条目数、已结算金额与验证点击

FAQ / 常见问题

What determines a listing’s position? / 什么决定条目的位置？

Position is determined by the settled sponsored total recorded for the public link.

位置由该公开链接已经结算的赞助总额决定。

Is the position a quality rating? / 这个位置代表质量评分吗？

No. It is a disclosed advertising placement, not an editorial review or quality assessment.

不是。这是明确标示的广告位置，不是编辑评价或质量评定。

Who can sponsor a link? / 谁可以赞助一个链接？

Anyone may sponsor an eligible public website or social profile. Payments accumulate on the link.

任何人都可以赞助符合条件的公开网站或社交账号，金额会累积在该链接上。

What information is public? / 哪些资料会公开？

The listing page shows its position, settled total, verified clicks and destination link.

条目页面会显示位置、已结算总额、验证点击及目标链接。

Does each market have a separate board? / 每个市场都有独立榜单吗？

Yes. Rankoff has 17 markets, each with its own ordering and published price for the first position.

是。Rankoff 设有 17 个市场，每个市场都有自己的排列及公开首位价格。
