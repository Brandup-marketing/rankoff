What is a pay-to-rank leaderboard?

A pay-to-rank leaderboard is a public advertising board where placement is determined by totals paid. Each website or social profile has a recorded sponsored total. Links with higher totals paid appear in higher positions.

The payment purchases an advertising placement at a published price. It does not represent an editorial recommendation, customer rating or assessment of business quality. Visitors can see that the placement is sponsored and can compare the recorded amounts directly.

Payments accumulate on the public link rather than the person making the payment. A business owner, customer, supporter or marketing agency may sponsor the same link. Each settled payment increases that link’s total. The payer does not receive ownership or editing rights over the website or social profile.

Rankoff is a public sponsored leaderboard for businesses, products and services across countries. An eligible public website or social profile can receive a listing. Browse software, design, marketing and other industries, alongside local shops and services. The live board shows the current entry price, currency and price to claim a position.

Every live listing receives a permanent Rankoff page showing its current position, sponsored total paid and tracked click count. The board is public and current: see the board for the current listings, total paid and tracked clicks

什么是付费排名赞助榜？

付费排名赞助榜是一种公开广告展示板。每个网站或社交账号都有一个累计赞助付款金额，金额较高的链接会显示在较前的位置。

付款购买的是以公开价格提供的广告位置，并不代表编辑推荐、客户评分或对商家质量的评定。Rankoff 会明确标示赞助性质，并公开显示每个链接的已结算金额。

赞助金额累积在公开链接上，而不是付款人名下。商家、客户、支持者或营销机构都可以赞助同一个链接。每笔成功结算的付款都会增加该链接的总额，但付款人不会因此取得网站或社交账号的所有权及编辑权。

Rankoff 是面向不同国家商家、产品与服务的公开付费赞助榜。符合条件的网站或公开社交主页可以上榜。访客可以探索软件、设计、营销等行业，以及本地店铺与服务。实时榜单会显示当前上榜价格、币种与取得某个位置的价格。

每个正式上榜链接都会获得一个永久 Rankoff 页面，公开显示当前位置、累计赞助付款金额及追踪点击数。榜单实时公开：请查看榜单上的最新条目数、已结算金额与追踪点击

FAQ / 常见问题

What determines a listing’s position? / 什么决定条目的位置？

Position is determined by the sponsored total paid recorded for the public link.

位置由该公开链接已经结算的赞助总额决定。

Is the position a quality rating? / 这个位置代表质量评分吗？

No. It is a disclosed advertising placement, not an editorial review or quality assessment.

不是。这是明确标示的广告位置，不是编辑评价或质量评定。

Who can sponsor a link? / 谁可以赞助一个链接？

Anyone may sponsor an eligible public website or social profile. Payments accumulate on the link.

任何人都可以赞助符合条件的公开网站或社交账号，金额会累积在该链接上。

What information is public? / 哪些资料会公开？

The listing page shows its position, total paid, tracked clicks and destination link.

条目页面会显示位置、累计付款金额、追踪点击及目标链接。

Does each industry have a separate board? / 每个行业都有独立榜单吗？

Yes. Rankoff has 19 industries, each with its own ordering and published price for the first position.

是。Rankoff 设有 19 个行业，每个行业都有自己的排列及公开首位价格。
