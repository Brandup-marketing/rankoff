How can Malaysian businesses get more online exposure?

Make it easy for people to find your business, understand what you offer and reach your website or social profile. Rankoff adds a public business listing and a sponsored position to that journey. The live board shows the current entry price and currency.

What does a Rankoff listing include?

A place on the public board and in your selected industry. Visitors can browse businesses by industry, open a listing and follow its website or social-profile link.

A shareable business page. It brings together your business name, description, destination link, current sponsored position, total paid and tracked clicks. Where sourced details are available, it also shows services and location.

A ranking card to share. Use the share control on your listing to create a card showing your position at that time, then share it with your own audience.

Help customers understand your business

Keep your public website or profile current: use a recognisable business name, describe your actual services, state your location or service area, and provide a working contact or booking route. Include opening hours when they are confirmed.

For examples, explore Hypex Auto’s automotive services in Wangsa Maju and Express Queen Lash’s eyelash services at The Strand KD. Their Rankoff pages link to the businesses’ own public sources.

How much does it cost, and how long does the listing stay?

The live board shows the current minimum and currency. Payments for the same destination accumulate, and the highest settled total ranks first in the selected industry and timeframe. The current price to claim a position appears before checkout. Being overtaken changes your position; it does not remove your listing.

How does this fit alongside social media and advertising?

Use your website or social profile for detailed information and enquiries, social posts to reach your followers, and ads when you want paid distribution. Your Rankoff page provides another public place to discover and share the business. The sponsored position is determined by payment, rather than a service-quality score.

Where can I see the response?

Open your listing to see its tracked clicks and ranking record. These clicks count redirects from Rankoff to your linked website or profile; repeat and automated traffic may be included. Use your own website or booking records to follow what visitors do after they arrive.

马来西亚商家怎样增加线上曝光？

让顾客更容易找到你的商家、了解你提供什么，并进入你的网站或社交主页。Rankoff 为这条发现路径增加公开商家页与赞助排名。实时榜单会显示当前上榜价格与币种。

上榜 Rankoff 会获得什么？

在公开榜单和所选行业中展示。访客可以按行业浏览商家、打开资料页，再进入商家的网站或社交主页。

一个可分享的商家资料页，集中显示名称、简介、目标链接、当前赞助排名、累计付款及追踪点击。有可核实资料的商家页也会显示服务和地点。

一张可以分享的排名卡。在商家页使用分享功能，生成显示当时排名的卡片，分享给你的受众。

让顾客看懂你的业务

保持公开网站或主页资料准确：使用可识别的商家名称，描述实际服务，列出地点或服务地区，并提供有效的联系或预约入口。营业时间确认后也应列明。

例如，可以查看 Hypex Auto 在 Wangsa Maju 的汽车服务，以及 Express Queen Lash 在 The Strand KD 的美睫服务。其 Rankoff 资料页均附有商家的公开资料来源。

多少钱，展示多久？

实时榜单会显示当前最低金额和币种。同一目标链接的付款会累积，在所选行业与时间范围内，累计已结算金额最高者排第一。取得某个位置的当前价格会在结账前显示。被超越会改变排名，不会让条目下架。

怎样配合社交媒体和广告？

用网站或社交主页提供详细资料并承接询问，用社交贴文触达关注者，需要付费分发时再配合广告。Rankoff 商家页提供另一个可被发现和分享的公开入口。赞助排名按付款金额决定，不是服务品质评分。

在哪里查看反馈？

打开商家页即可查看追踪点击与排名记录。点击统计从 Rankoff 跳转至目标网站或主页的次数，可能包含重复与自动化流量。访客抵达后的行为，可以通过你自己的网站或预约记录继续了解。
