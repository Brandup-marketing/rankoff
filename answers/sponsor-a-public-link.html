How can I sponsor a website or social profile?

Rankoff allows anyone to purchase advertising placement for an eligible public website or social profile. The process starts with the public destination link, not the identity of the payer.

Enter a public link. Submit the website or public social-profile address that should appear on Rankoff. Select its market. Choose the market that describes the business or activity; the platform carrying the link does not determine its category. Review the placement. Rankoff shows the published advertising price and the position associated with the payment. Complete payment. Placements start from RM5, and only settled amounts are included in the listing total. View the public record. The listing receives a permanent page showing its position, settled total and verified clicks.

Payments accumulate on the normalized link. If a website or social profile is already listed, another settled payment increases the same listing’s sponsored total. It does not create a second record for the same normalized destination.

The payer may be the business owner, a customer, a supporter or an agency. Payment does not provide access to the destination account and does not permit the payer to edit its profile information. Rankoff presents the placement as sponsored advertising.

Rankoff is new, and the figures on the board are the real ones: see the board for the current listings, settled total and verified clicks

如何赞助网站或社交账号？

任何人都可以通过 Rankoff，为符合条件的公开网站或社交账号购买广告位置。系统以公开目标链接为基础，不以付款人的身份建立排名。

输入公开链接：提交希望展示在 Rankoff 的网站或公开社交账号网址。选择所属市场：按照业务或活动类型选择市场，使用哪个社交平台不会自动决定商业分类。检查广告位置：Rankoff 会显示已公布的广告价格及付款对应的位置。完成付款：广告位置从 RM5 起，只有成功结算的金额才会加入条目总额。查看公开记录：条目会获得永久页面，显示当前位置、已结算总额和验证点击。

付款会累积在经过标准化处理的链接上。如果该网站或社交账号已经上榜，新的已结算付款会增加同一个条目的赞助总额，不会为同一个标准化目标建立重复记录。

付款人可以是商家、客户、支持者或营销机构。付款不会获得目标账号的登入权限，也不能修改其资料。Rankoff 会把该位置明确标示为赞助广告。

Rankoff 目前仍是一个新平台，榜单上的数字都是真实记录：请查看榜单上的最新条目数、已结算金额与验证点击

FAQ / 常见问题

Must I own the website or social profile? / 我必须拥有该网站或社交账号吗？

No. Anyone may sponsor an eligible public link. Payment does not provide ownership or editing rights.

不需要。任何人都可以赞助符合条件的公开链接，但付款不会获得所有权或编辑权。

What is the starting amount? / 起始金额是多少？

Rankoff advertising placements start from RM5. The applicable published price is shown before payment.

Rankoff 广告位置从 RM5 起，付款前会显示适用的公开价格。

Can I sponsor an existing listing? / 我可以赞助已经存在的条目吗？

Yes. A settled payment increases the sponsored total attached to that normalized link.

可以。成功结算的付款会增加该标准化链接的赞助总额。

How should I choose a market? / 应该如何选择市场？

Choose the market that describes the business or activity, regardless of the platform carrying the destination.

按照业务或活动类型选择市场，不需要根据承载目标链接的平台决定。

What happens after settlement? / 付款结算后会怎样？

The public board updates and the listing page records its position, settled total and verified clicks.

公开榜单会更新，条目页面会记录位置、已结算总额和验证点击。
