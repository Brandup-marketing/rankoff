# Rankoff founder recruitment kit

Prepared 8 September 2026. Drafts for Jake to review and use; no invitations, posts or ads have been sent. This is a practical outreach kit, not another software feature.

## The four launch needs

1. **Founding cohort:** recruit SaaS, AI-tool and digital-business founders from one relevant community. The experiment target is 20 independently paying businesses in 30 days, with evidence of repeat payment. These are targets, not forecasts or current results.
2. **Useful discovery:** visitors can understand what a product does and who it helps, then visit it. A position alone does not prove useful traffic.
3. **Measurable acquisition:** connect tagged visits, opened payment reviews, created checkouts, settled payments, repeat payments and outbound clicks. Judge the experiment on payment and discovery evidence, not ad reactions.
4. **Reasons to return:** manually share real new listings and ranking changes with the same community. Publish only when there is something factual to report.

## First audience and offer

Choose one founder community where Jake can participate and promotion is allowed. Prefer founders with a working product that serves customers across countries. Malaysia remains welcome; geography is not the limit.

Offer a public sponsored listing with transparent ranking and tracked outbound clicks. Do not promise sales, a fixed position, a minimum audience or guaranteed traffic. All paying participants follow the same ranking rules. There is no invented founding badge, free credit, prize or exclusive benefit.

Use the price actually live on the landing page. At preparation, the production launch state last inspected was MYR, with RM5 entry. US$1 is unpublished preparation and must not appear in published copy until Dodo USD checkout and the legacy-value policy have been verified and released. Recheck the live board immediately before posting. Entry price is not necessarily the price of overall #1.

## One follow-up, if appropriate

## Discovery checklist for each participating business

- Does its real title identify the product?
- Does its description state a function and intended user? Useful pattern: “[Product] helps [audience] do [specific job].” This is a writing pattern, not permission to invent missing product facts.
- Is the destination correct, public and usable?
- Is the market appropriate?
- Can the founder distinguish total paid, current position and the price of moving up?

Website descriptions currently come from available public metadata. Ask founders to improve their own metadata when it is vague. Social profiles may lack metadata; do not fabricate a bio or retry the blocked social-fetch workflow. Any manual owner correction must be based on information supplied or verified by the business.

## Manual update templates

**New listing:** “[Product] joined Rankoff in [market]. It helps [verified audience] [verified function]. Current sponsored position: #[rank] in [scope], [timeframe], checked [date/time and timezone]. Discover it: [listing URL].”

**Ranking change:** “[Product] moved from #[old rank] to #[new rank] in [same scope and timeframe]. Checked [date/time and timezone]. The order follows total settled payment. See the current board: [URL].”

Use a recorded before/after snapshot for a rank-change claim. If no change occurred, do not manufacture an update. No automatic messages or scheduled posts are enabled by this kit.

## Creative order

1. A current real leaderboard screenshot. Keep the board scope, timeframe, currency, total-paid labels and current claim price visible. Record its capture date. Never use synthetic local fixtures as launch proof.
2. The brand concept image. The updated generic social preview says “Claim your position”; it contains no price or fabricated traction.

Publish screenshots only when their prices agree with the live page. State “#1 in [market]” when it is a market rank. Do not suggest an overall rank from a category screenshot. Refresh creatives after the USD switch.

## 30-day routine and decision

Days 1–3: select the community, check its posting rules, prepare the private prospect list, verify live pricing and tracking. Start the 30-day clock when recruitment begins, not when this document was written.

Days 4–14: have individual founder conversations, publish the approved post where allowed, record objections and any settled listings. Inspect actual outbound traffic with customers where they are willing to share it.

Days 15–30: follow up once where appropriate, share real activity and review payment/return behavior. Keep own-network support separate from independent demand.

Record weekly: qualifying businesses paid, new settled payments, repeat payments, eligible campaign visits, attributed checkouts, outbound clicks, reported usefulness and actual acquisition spend. Cost per independently paying business = actual spend / qualifying businesses; when none have paid, report “no acquisition yet,” not a zero cost.

At day 30, compare the evidence with the 20-business experiment target. If founders understand the offer but decline its value, improve distribution or the proposition before building more features. If people pay but see little useful discovery, focus on relevant visitors. Repeat payment is a positive signal; it is not proof of customer revenue.

## Measurement limits

The new owner report is local preparation until migration 0007 and the code are released. It uses the first campaign source within a browser-tab session lasting up to 30 minutes. Campaign rows group sessions started in the last 30 days and the payments tied to those sessions; the coverage line separately counts payments settled in the last 30 days. Refreshes do not add visits within the session. Browser events and tags are visitor supplied and can include bots. Blocked storage, another tab/device, missing tags and older checkouts can leave payment attribution missing.

Repeat payment means an additional still-settled payment for the same listing, even if its first payment was earlier or in MYR. It does not identify a unique returning person. Reversed payments are excluded. Outbound clicks are redirects, including repeat and automated traffic. They are not unique people, leads or sales. No currency totals are combined in the campaign report.
